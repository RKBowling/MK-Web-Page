Kharacter Bio

Scorpion, once, was a man named Hanzo Hasashi: leader of the Shirai Ryu, til one day there was a suprise attacked that wiped out his entire clan including himself. In death, he is reborn as a spectre and vows to take revenge on Sub Zero.

Fun Facts
